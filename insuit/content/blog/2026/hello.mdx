The blog starts here. Each post is an MDX file in `content/blog/<year>/`, its
name the address: this one is `2026/hello.mdx`, at `/blog/hello`. It is hidden,
so the list leaves it out; it stays for the tests, and to show what a post can
hold.

## A video

MDX is markdown that takes JSX, so a post can hold a component. This video, for
one, loads nothing from YouTube until it is played. You have been warned:

## Pictures

The pictures here are stock photos from Unsplash, served by Picsum
(picsum.photos), so the post carries no picture of its own. A single picture is
plain markdown, its caption the title in quotes:

![A deep blue fjord between grey cliffs, seen from high on a rock ledge](https://picsum.photos/id/1015/1600/1067 "A fjord. Photo: Alexey Topolyanskiy, Unsplash")

step through the rest:
